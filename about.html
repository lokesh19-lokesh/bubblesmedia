Our Story

How Bubbles Media became Hyderabad's trusted digital marketing partner

Bubbles Media — Powered by The Patterns Company

Bubbles Media is a dedicated digital marketing subsidiary of The Patterns Company, created with a clear mission: to make high-quality digital marketing accessible, affordable, and trustworthy for everyone.

In today’s world, having a strong online presence is no longer optional—it is essential. However, digital marketing has become increasingly expensive, leaving many startups and growing businesses behind. Bubbles Media was born to solve this problem.

We focus purely on delivering result-driven digital marketing services, including:

  • Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
  • Social Media Marketing (SMM)
  • Meta Ads & Google Ads
  • Affordable, high-quality websites
  • Complete online presence solutions

Our goal is simple: empower small startups, growing businesses, and established companies to build a powerful digital identity without financial barriers.

Backed by 8+ Years of Excellence

Bubbles Media is powered by the strong foundation of The Patterns Company, which brings over 8 years of tremendous experience in the technology and digital ecosystem.

Our parent company has successfully served:

  • Hundreds of clients across industries
  • Public limited companies
  • Film production houses
  • Government initiatives such as Telangana PM KUSUM project
  • Prestigious organizations like the Indian Dental Association
  • And many more enterprise-level clients

With proven expertise, experienced teams, and strong systems, we deliver the same enterprise-level quality through Bubbles Media at accessible pricing.
